C3Key facts

Weekly statusActivity = hot VIIRS nights (375 m) over clear nights (ERA5 cloud cover < 0.5) in an 8-week window, compared with the same weeks (± 2) of the 3 previous years. Ratio below 0.6: stopped. Fewer than 5 clear nights or too weak a reference: unknown. Series start in March 2019, the first week with a reference.

OutputQuarterly estimate = published rate of the last 12 months (else 80 % of capacity) × activity factor (r if r < 0.6, else 1), computed without looking ahead. Mean error 8.0 % vs 11.1 % for the naive forecast on 99 published figures.

Output alertsA surprise is a published figure more than 10 % away from the naive forecast. The alert comes from VIIRS (r < 0.6 or > 1.3), else from the share of hot images (< 0.75 or > 1.33), thresholds fixed in advance.
